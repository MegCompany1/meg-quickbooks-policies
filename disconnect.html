Disconnect QuickBooks

MEG QuickBooks Integration · Meg Company Inc.

If you disconnected the MEG QuickBooks Integration from QuickBooks Online, the disconnection is complete. The integration can no longer read new data from QuickBooks.

To disconnect

A QuickBooks Online administrator for MEG can disconnect the integration at any time from the apps/connected apps area of QuickBooks Online, or by emailing tech@megcompany.com.

Data already downloaded

Data previously downloaded to MEG computers can be deleted on request to tech@megcompany.com. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Reconnect

To connect again, see Connect or reconnect QuickBooks.

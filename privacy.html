Privacy Policy

MEG QuickBooks Integration · Meg Company Inc. · Effective October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Meg Company Inc. ("MEG", "we") handles data accessed through the MEG QuickBooks Integration (the "Integration"), a private, internal tool that connects MEG's own QuickBooks Online company to Claude Code. The Integration is used only by authorized MEG staff and contractors.

1. Data we access

With MEG's authorization through Intuit, the Integration reads data from MEG's QuickBooks Online company, which may include:

  • Company information and chart of accounts
  • Customers and vendors (names, contact and billing details as recorded in QuickBooks)
  • Transactions such as invoices, payments, deposits, bills, bill payments and journal entries
  • Financial reports such as profit and loss and balance sheet

The Integration only reads data. It does not create, change or delete QuickBooks records.

2. How we use it

We use this data only for MEG's internal business purposes: budgeting, cash planning, financial analysis and reconciling QuickBooks with MEG's other internal business systems. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

3. Storage

  • QuickBooks authorization credentials (such as OAuth client secrets and access tokens) are kept in environment variables or a local configuration file on authorized MEG computers. They are excluded from source code repositories.
  • Accounting data downloaded from QuickBooks is saved in local working folders on authorized MEG computers. Those folders are excluded from source code repositories.
  • The Integration's program code is kept in a private source code repository that does not contain raw accounting data or credentials.

4. Access by staff and service providers

Access to the Integration and the data it retrieves is limited to authorized MEG staff and contractors who need it for their work.

The Integration is used through Claude Code, a tool provided by Anthropic. When staff use Claude Code to work with QuickBooks data, the data included in those requests is transmitted to Anthropic to be processed. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. QuickBooks data is also held by Intuit as part of the QuickBooks Online service, under Intuit's terms and privacy policy.

We do not otherwise share data accessed through the Integration with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Disconnecting the Integration

A QuickBooks Online administrator for MEG can disconnect the Integration at any time from the apps/connected apps area of QuickBooks Online, or by contacting us. Once disconnected, the Integration can no longer read new data from QuickBooks. Data previously downloaded to MEG computers can be deleted on request to the contact below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page with a new effective date.
